The first release. AirFocus reads your AirPods head tracking and focuses the window you're facing, so you can move between terminals without touching the mouse.

- Focus follows your head after a short hold, and holds off while you type.
- Flick up for Mission Control, then hold your head on a window to pick it.
- Flick left or right to move one Desktop.
- Look away for 5 seconds to start the screen saver. Look back for the unlock prompt.
- Nothing draws on screen by default. A visible overlay with your own colours and shapes is a setting away, and the menu bar icon can hide too.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26, and AirPods with head tracking. Install with:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabesgh/airfocus/main/install.sh | bash
```

The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID. If you download `AirFocus.zip` from this page instead, allow it once under System Settings › Privacy & Security.
